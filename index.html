Creating passwords for new accounts

This is a short study by three Software Engineering students of group PI25E at Vilniaus kolegija (Mohamed Arkik, Jamal El-Mazzioui and Brahim Daali) for the course Applied Research Methodology.

What you will do. Create passwords for two imaginary accounts, read a short text and answer two short questions. It takes about 5 minutes.

Important. Do not type any password that you use for a real account. Make new ones, and do not use them anywhere after the study.

What we save. A random code, numbers about each password (estimated strength and length), your answers to the two questions, whether you pasted a password and whether you left this page while reading the text. Your passwords are checked inside your browser and are never saved or sent. We do not ask for your name or email.

Your rights. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing this page, and it has no effect on your grades. The answers are kept in a private file that only our team and our lecturer can open. We will delete them after the course ends.

Please take part only once and, if you can, use a computer. Questions? Message any of us on Microsoft Teams.
