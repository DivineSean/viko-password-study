Creating passwords for new accounts

A short study by three Software Engineering students of group PI25E for the course Applied Research Methodology. It takes about 5 minutes.

What you will do

Create passwords for two imaginary accounts, read a short text and answer two short questions. It works best on a computer.

Important

Do not type any password that you use for a real account. Make new ones, and do not use them anywhere after the study.

What we save

A random code, numbers about each password (estimated strength and length), your answers to two questions, whether you pasted a password and whether you left this page while reading. Your passwords are checked inside your browser and are never saved or sent. We do not ask for your name or email.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing this page, and it has no effect on your grades. Only our team and our lecturer can see the answers, and we delete them after the course ends.
